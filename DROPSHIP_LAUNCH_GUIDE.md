# Ichiban Drop Shipping - Complete Launch Guide

## 🎯 Overview
This guide walks you through launching your drop shipping business from finding suppliers to fulfilling your first order.

---

## Phase 1: Find & Vet Suppliers (Week 1-2)

### Step 1: Search for Suppliers

**Platform Options:**
1. **Alibaba** (alibaba.com)
   - Best for: Larger orders, better pricing
   - Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): Usually 50-100+ units
   - Good for: Once you validate demand

2. **AliExpress** (aliexpress.com)
   - Best for: Testing, smaller orders
   - MOQ: Often just 1 unit
   - Good for: Getting started quickly

3. **DHgate** (dhgate.com)
   - Best for: Sports apparel, no MOQ
   - Good middle ground between Alibaba and AliExpress

### Step 2: Search Terms to Use
```
- "BJJ rashguard custom logo"
- "MMA fight shorts custom embroidery"
- "BJJ gi custom branding"
- "Sublimation rashguard"
- "Blank rashguard for customization"
```

### Step 3: What to Look For in Suppliers

**Quality Indicators:**
- ✅ 4+ star rating
- ✅ 1000+ transactions
- ✅ "Gold Supplier" badge (Alibaba)
- ✅ Trade Assurance (protection)
- ✅ Response time < 24 hours
- ✅ Good English communication

**Red Flags:**
- ❌ New supplier (< 6 months)
- ❌ Low ratings or bad reviews
- ❌ No custom logo option
- ❌ Prices too good to be true
- ❌ Poor communication

### Step 4: Contact 5-10 Suppliers

**Message Template:**
```
Hello,

I'm looking to order custom Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu gear with my gym logo for my academy in Honduras.

Products needed:
- Rashguards (compression, sublimated logo)
- Fight Shorts (embroidered logo)
- BJJ Gi (complete kimono with embroidered patches)

Initial order: 10-20 units for testing

- Product catalog
- Price list with customization
- Sample photos
- Shipping options to Honduras

Ichiban Jiu Jitsu Academy
Tegucigalpa, Honduras
```

## Phase 2: Order Samples (Week 2-3)

### Step 5: Narrow Down to 2-3 Suppliers

**Compare:**

### Step 6: Create Your Logo Files

**You Need:**
1. **High-Resolution Logo** (PNG, 300 DPI minimum)
   - White version (for dark fabrics)
   - Black version (for light fabrics)
   - Color version

2. **Vector Logo** (AI, EPS, or SVG)
   - Required for embroidery
   - Scalable without quality loss

**Where to Get:**
- If you have a logo: Convert to vector at fiverr.com ($5-20)
- Need a logo: Hire designer on Fiverr/Upwork ($20-100)

### Step 7: Order 1 Sample of Each Product

**Sample Order:**
- 1x Rashguard with your logo (Medium size)
- 1x Shorts with your logo (Medium size)
- 1x Gi with your logo (A2 size)

**Cost Estimate:**
- Products: $30-60
- Customization: $10-20
- Shipping: $20-40
- **Total: ~$80-120**

**Specify:**
- Logo placement (chest, sleeves, back)
- Logo size (ask for recommendations)
- Colors you want
- Your shipping address

## Phase 4: Negotiate & Set Up Production (Week 4-5)

### Step 10: Choose Your Supplier

**Select Based On:**
1. Best sample quality (60% weight)
2. Communication & reliability (20%)
3. Price & speed (20%)

### Step 11: Negotiate Terms

**Negotiate:**
```
1. Bulk Pricing
   - "If I order 50 units, what's the unit price?"
   - "100 units?"
   - Aim for 20-30% discount on bulk

2. Repeat Order Discount
   - "I'll order monthly, can you give better rates?"

3. Faster Production
   - "Can you prioritize my orders?"
   - "What's rush fee for 7-day production?"

4. Payment Terms
   - Start: 100% upfront
   - Later: 50% deposit, 50% before shipping

5. Quality Guarantee
   - "What if quality doesn't match sample?"
   - Get written guarantee
```

### Step 12: Set Up Payment Method

**Options for International Suppliers:**

1. **PayPal Business** (Recommended for starting)
   - Buyer protection
   - Easy to use
   - ~4% fee

2. **Alibaba Trade Assurance**
   - Built-in protection
   - Escrow service
   - Safe for larger orders

3. **Bank Transfer / Wire**
   - Lowest fees
   - Use only after trust established
   - Get written agreement first

4. **Western Union** (Not recommended)
   - No protection
   - Only if absolutely necessary

3. Go through checkout
4. Make payment (you refund them)
5. You manually process it

**Test:**
- Does checkout work?
- Do emails send?
- Can you track the order?
- What's customer experience?

### Step 17: Create Order Processing Workflow

**Document Your Process:**

1. **Customer Orders on Website**
   - You receive email notification
   - Order stored in database

2. **You Contact Supplier**
   - Send order details
   - Customer name + address
   - Product + size + logo placement
   - Pay supplier

3. **Supplier Produces**
   - 7-14 days production
   - Send you photos for approval

4. **Supplier Ships**
   - Get tracking number
   - Update customer via email

5. **Customer Receives**
   - 8-14 days shipping
   - Total: 15-28 days

## Emergency Contacts & Resources

### Shipping to Honduras

**Major Carriers:**
1. **DHL Express**
   - Fast (5-7 days)
   - Expensive
   - Reliable tracking

2. **EMS (China Post)**
   - Medium speed (10-15 days)
   - Good value
   - OK tracking

3. **ePacket**
   - Slow (15-25 days)
   - Cheapest
   - Spotty tracking

4. **Air Freight**
   - Best for bulk (50+ units)
   - 7-12 days
   - Cost-effective at scale

### Honduras Customs Info

**Import Duties:**
- Textiles/Clothing: ~15-20% of value
- Must declare true value
- May need importation permit for commercial quantities

**Tips:**
- Declare items as "samples" for small orders
- Use personal address for small shipments
- Consider using customs broker for bulk

### Payment Processing (Honduras)

**For Your Website:**

1. **BAC Credomatic** (Already in your code!)
   - Local bank in Honduras
   - Accept Visa/Mastercard
   - ~3% fee
   - Setup: Visit bank branch

2. **PayPal**
   - Easy setup
   - ~5% fee
   - Customers need PayPal account

3. **Bank Transfer / Deposit**
   - No fees
   - Manual process
   - Good for gym members

## Success Tips

### DO:
